## Goal

The first of v0.2's dice (`docs/roadmap.md`): a D8, rolled one to three at a time like the D6 and
the D20, and passing invariant #2's fairness test before it ships (ADR-0002, ADR-0009). It goes
first because it needs nothing new from the code that reads and draws a die: its faces are
triangles, as the D20's are, and its result is the face lying flattest on top. The D12's
pentagons, the D10's kites and the D4's top vertex each bring a design question of their own and
get slices of their own.

## Definition of Done

- **Given** the octahedron `makeD8` builds
- **When** the geometry test checks it as it checks the D6 and the D20
- **Then** it is closed and wound outward, every edge has the requested length, its numerals run 1
  to 8 with opposite faces summing to 9, and its inertia matches the integrated body

- **Given** one D8, then three at a time, rolled one roll after another on the recorded shakes
- **When** the fairness test tallies 5,100 results
- **Then** chi-square stays under 24.322 (7 degrees of freedom, α = 0.001), the share of results
  on the die's previous face passes against 1 in 8, and every roll comes to rest

- **Given** the rest and cocked thresholds were measured on the D6 and the D20 only
- **When** the fairness probe traces 2,000 D8 rolls
- **Then** the longest false rest stays under a quarter of `REST_HOLD_S`, and the flattest propped
  D8 tilts more than `COCKED_TILT_RAD` while the most tilted flat one tilts less; if either fails,
  the threshold is re-chosen from the measured numbers before the D8 ships

- **Given** any choice of die type
- **When** PRIMARY is pressed (Enter on the Cardputer, button A on the StickC)
- **Then** the type steps D6, D8, D20 and back to D6, and the count stays as it was

- **Given** a board flashed with this build
- **When** the D8 is picked and the device is shaken
- **Then** the band reads `D8` before the roll, the dice tumble and each lands showing the numeral
  the band reports for it

## Design

**Size.** The D8's edge is 14.1 mm, so that its vertices lie on the same 10.0 mm sphere as the
D20's (a D20 of edge 10.5 mm has circumradius 10.5 × 0.951 = 10.0 mm; an octahedron's is a/√2).
The dice of a set then look one size, and dice collide by their bounding spheres (ADR-0002), so
dice-to-dice contact behaves as it does for the D20. The D8 is drawn with an edge of
14.1 × 2.29 = 32 px, larger than ADR-0007's 24 px for the D20, so its numerals read at least as
well. Considered and not taken: the D20's 10.5 mm edge, which would make the D8 a 14.8 mm die next
to a 20 mm one.

**Shape.** Vertices at ±s on each axis, with s = a/√2; one face per octant, wound outward. The
numerals put 1 to 4 on the faces with z > 0 and 9 − n on the face opposite each, as
`DieFace::numeral` already requires. A uniform octahedron of edge a has I = m a² / 10. It has 6
vertices and 8 faces, inside `DIE_MAX_VERTICES` and `DIE_MAX_FACES`, and 3 corners per face, inside
`FACE_MAX_CORNERS`.

**Choosing it.** `DieType` gains `D8` between `D6` and `D20`, and PRIMARY steps to the next type in
the enum's order, wrapping round. The four dice still to come slot into the same order (D4 before
D6, D10 and D12 before D20), so the rule never changes again. That is up to five presses on the
StickC once there are six types; dice notation covers the Cardputer later (`docs/roadmap.md`).
Considered and not taken: putting D20 first, which keeps today's D20-to-D6 press but leaves an
order no player would guess.

**Fairness.** Slice 0008's sizing, one face 1.25× too likely caught with 99% power at α = 0.001,
reproduced from the noncentral chi-square with the other faces sharing the shortfall: 3,370 D6
results (it shipped 3,400) and 17,940 D20 results (18,000). The same calculation for 8 faces gives
5,090, so the D8 test takes 5,100 results, against a critical value of 24.322 at 7 degrees of
freedom.

**Thresholds measured on other dice.** `REST_HOLD_S` is four times the longest pause after which a
D6 or a D20 still tipped, and `COCKED_TILT_RAD` sits between the flat and propped tilts the D6 and
D20 reached (`src/physics/physics_config.h`, slice 0008). Neither holds for a D8 by argument: an
octahedron's faces meet at a sharper dihedral angle (109.5°, against the D20's 138.2°), so it
tips differently and props differently. The fairness probe's `trace` mode measures both, and it
gains `D8` as a die name.

**What existing code was considered.** `makeD20`'s `triangleFace` builds a triangular `DieFace`
and is reused for the D8. The D20's mirror-through-the-centre construction is not: an
octahedron's octants give each face directly, and its sign pattern is simpler to read than half a
face list. `shapeOf` in `src/main.cpp` and `parseShape` in the probe each gain a case; at three
types that is two branches each, short of the rule of three, so no table of shapes is added yet.
The D12 slice, the fourth type, is where one would earn its place.

## Verification

- `test/test_geometry`: the D8 passes `assertRegularSolid`, as the D6 and the D20 do (`npm test`).
- `test/test_fairness`: one D8 at a time in `npm test`, three at a time in `npm run test:fairness`
  (invariant #2).
- `test/test_dice_choice`: PRIMARY steps D6, D8, D20, D6, and `dieTypeName` names the D8.
- `scripts/fairness-probe.mjs` with `trace D8 2000 1`: the longest false rest and the flat and
  propped tilts, recorded below. A probe answers its question once; it is not a verify script.
- `scripts/roll-verify.mjs` on both boards (`npm run verify:roll`): it rolls three D8s as it rolls
  three D6s and three D20s, through the product's serial commands, so every roll must lock and the
  D8's on-device results pass its chi-square. Reaching a type now takes one `T` per step through
  the cycle, which `scripts/roll-verify.test.mjs` checks. Its screen capture after the D8 rolls is
  compared with the band by eye: a rendered die's numeral cannot be asserted in a unit test.

## As built

The D8 shipped as designed: `makeD8` at a 14.1 mm edge, derived in `physics_config.h` from the
D20's, and PRIMARY stepping D6, D8, D20 by the enum's order.

**The hold did not survive the D8, and was raised.** Over 2,000 traced D8 rolls, the longest pause
below 5 mm/s and 0.5 rad/s after which a die still tipped was 0.0417 s (10 steps; the next longest
0.0333 s). ADR-0009's 0.15 s hold outlasted it only 3.6 times over, against the four ADR-0009
chose. `REST_HOLD_S` became 0.17 s, ADR-0009 carries the amendment at that claim, and
`scripts/fairness-probe.mjs` now fails any die whose traced pause the hold does not outlast four
times over: the probe program prints the product's thresholds with its trace grid, so the check
reads the hold the physics ran with. The cocked tilt held: the D8 lay flat at most 0.066° and
propped at least 1.874°, either side of 0.5°.

**Fairness, at the 0.17 s hold** (`node scripts/fairness-probe.mjs`, 10 min 56 s on 12 cores,
every check passed, D6 and D20 included):

| D8 run | Results | Chi-square p (lowest of 3 seeds) | On the previous face (chance 12.5%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tally from rest, one die | 5,100 | 0.581 | |
| Tally from rest, three dice | 5,100 | 0.198 | |
| Chained on shakes, one die | 5,100 | 0.131 | 12.16 to 12.53% |
| Chained on shakes, three dice | 5,100 | 0.348 | 12.49 to 12.92% |

Replays with the product's kick, pooled by kind of shake, came back on the previous face 11.99%
(slow, p 0.075) to 12.74% (hard). `npm test` and `npm run test:fairness` pass the D8 one and three
at a time.

**On the devices** (`npm run verify:roll`, 40 rolls of three D8 on each board, 2026-11-23): every
roll locked and none was knocked over after locking. The D8's chi-square p was 0.668 on the StickC
and 0.310 on the Cardputer, its longest roll 1.27 s and 1.12 s, and its four physics steps cost at
most 3.6 ms and 3.1 ms, under the D20's; no frame was late. Both captures after the D8 rolls showed
each die's numeral matching the band (6, 5, 5 on the StickC; 1, 3, 6 on the Cardputer), and the
StickC's became the README's D8 picture.
